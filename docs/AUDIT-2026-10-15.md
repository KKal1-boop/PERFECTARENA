# AUDIT — 2026-10-15

The first measurement of the owner's own complaints, taken on the real install
(`C:/ArenaProject/arena/ARENA`) in headless Chrome, against the tree at commit `2986324`
plus the audit suite written in this pass. **Nothing in this document is inferred**: every
number is printed by a command, and every claim that could not be measured says so.

```bash
ARENA_PATH=C:/ArenaProject/arena/ARENA npm test                 # 412/412 unit
ARENA_PATH=C:/ArenaProject/arena/ARENA node tests/run-browser.mjs audit
```

**Result of the audit run: 14 of 23 checks pass, 9 fail.** The suite is *meant* to be red —
each failure is a work item, and each is filed in `docs/DEFECTS.md`. It lives in
`tests/suites/audit.mjs` and is registered as `audit` in `tests/run-browser.mjs`.

What the audit walked, in one run (~6 minutes): Daggerfall at 10:00 and at 23:00, the smith
itself (`EQUIP2.MIF`), three wilderness windows — one per climate, at the same grid cell
`(1024, 1024)` — Daggerfall's country at 23:00, and the Imperial Dungeons.

The three settlements were picked from the data, not from memory: `TERRAIN.IMG`'s own class
at each one's pixel is desert for Hammerfell's **Rihad** (131 settlements sit on desert),
mountain for Valenwood's **Eldenroot** (52) and temperate for High Rock's **Daggerfall**
(105). `?climate=` does **not** change a wilderness window's `.INF` — only `place.climate`
does — so a settled name is the only honest way to ask a page for a climate.

## S-A — "NPCs that can't move" — **the complaint is half right**

| context | people | moved > 5 m in 20 s | mid-step | on a route | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Daggerfall, 10:00 | 48 (48 figures drawn) | **43 (90 %)** | 17 | 7 | the street walks |
| Daggerfall, 23:00 | 48 | **0** | 0 | 0 | **RED — a frozen crowd** |
| the smith (`EQUIP2.MIF`), 10:00 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 | correct: a keeper behind a counter stands |
| wilderness, temperate / desert / mountain | **0** | 0 | 0 | 0 | **RED — the country is uninhabited** |

* **In town, in daylight, people walk.** 43 of 48 were more than two cells (`CELL_SIZE` =
  2.5 m) from where they were, `animation().moving` reported live walk cycles, and 7 of them
  were mid-route to a named place. The previous session's work holds.
* **At 23:00 the town is not asleep, it is frozen.** The same 48 people are still on the
  pavement with 0 movement in 20 s. The correct shape is fewer people, most of them *indoors*
  (or walking home with a walk cycle), which is what `dayPlanFor`'s `goHome`/`sleep` are for.
  Not yet root-caused: whether `stepTownsfolk` gates movement at night, or the route book
  refuses to start a walk after dark, or the crowd is standing in a "sleep" activity that has
  no destination. **File as D-01a and probe before fixing.**
* **The country has no people at all**, and this one *is* root-caused by reading the code:
  `populate()` (`interior.js`) computes `inhabited = inCity || entryCell` and its final
  `else { townsfolk = []; closeTalk(); }` empties the list for every wilderness window and
  every dungeon. There is no traveller, no farmer, no guard on the road — nothing that could
  walk. **D-01b.**

## S-B — "missing enemies" — **confirmed, with the exact cause**

| context | creatures | kinds | roster source |
|---|---|---|---|
| wilderness, temperate (10:00) | **0** | `{}` | `TWN.INF` → **roster `[]`** |
| wilderness, desert (10:00) | **0** | `{}` | `DWN.INF` → **roster `[]`** |
| wilderness, mountain (10:00) | **0** | `{}` | `MWN.INF` → **roster `[]`** |
| wilderness, temperate (23:00) | **0** | `{}` | same |
| Imperial Dungeons (`START.MIF`) | 5 | stonegolem, ghoul, goblin, firedaemon, vampire | its own `.INF`: 23 species, rat → lich |

**Root cause, from the bytes.** The wilderness `.INF`s place only three item flats —
`itemIndexes [15, 2, 14]` (measured with `monsterRosterFromInf` over `TWN.INF`, `DWN.INF`,
`MWN.INF`) — and **none of them is in the monster range**, so `monsterRosterFromInf` returns
`[]`, `monsterCountFor` returns 0 by its own law ("a level that names no monster of its own
gets none"), and the country is *empty by construction*. Two consequences:

* P1's wilderness bestiary (per terrain, per time of day, packs, wildlife, night spawns) is
  not an enhancement — it is the only thing that can put anything in the country at all.
  OpenTESArena/UESP say Arena kept wilderness encounters in the executable; until ACD.EXE is
  unpacked (P2) the table is **this project's own** and must be stamped as such.
* **A comment in the code is wrong and must be retired**: `game/encounters.js` says
  "`start.inf` — the Imperial Dungeons — lists keys, equipment and treasure and not one
  creature, which is exactly why the escape is quiet". Measured, `start.inf` names **23
  species** (itemIndexes 1…8 and 32…54) and the Imperial Dungeons field five of them. The
  law ("a level that names none gets none") stays; the sentence justifying it does not.
  **D-02b.**
* Dungeons themselves are healthy: 5 creatures drawn from the level's own 23-species roster,
  per-actor kinds — the "most monsters dont spawn but skeletons and spiders" defect of the
  previous pass is genuinely fixed where it was fixed.

## S-C — "missing textures/assets" — **clean on every level walked; coverage is the real gap**

`state().missingTextures` — every sub-mesh whose texture is not in the renderer — was
**empty on all seven levels walked**: Daggerfall's city, the smith's interior, three
wilderness windows and the Imperial Dungeons. `scenery.missing` (the level's own flats) was 0
and `wild.missingBlocks` was empty on all three windows. Daggerfall's city draws with 15
textures, the dungeons likewise.

The honest limits of that green:

* **Seven levels is not 288 settlements.** This is a regression probe, not a coverage proof.
* The archive-wide picture is unchanged and is where the user's complaint lives
  (`node tools/assetcoverage.mjs`): of **2 441 `GLOBAL.BSA` records, 156 (6.4 %) are named in
  source, 594 (24.3 %) are reachable through a composed name, and 1 691 are named by nothing**.
  Loose in the install: 325 files, 54 named whole. Census — `img:946 mif:533 cfa:340 set:180
  inf:93 dfa:79 voc:76 rmd:70 cif:58 xfm:33 xmi:33`; the largest unread family is the speech
  bank (`*.VOC`, 64 KB each).
* **946 `.IMG` screens, 180 `.SET` tilesets, 340 `.CFA` creatures and 79 `.DFA` fixtures are
  still largely unused** — that, not a broken texture, is what "missing assets" means.
* The renderer's "a missing texture draws untextured, not black" behaviour means
  `missingTextures` is the only thing that *notices* a miss; there is still no counter for a
  flat or a font that silently fell back.

## S-D — "missing landscapes/backgrounds" — **NOT confirmed at this depth; the climates differ**

The brief's premise was "a desert city and a jungle city are two block grids with different
palettes". Measured at the same wilderness cell `(1024, 1024)`:

| climate | mean luminance | distinct colours | `.RMD` blocks placed | flats placed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| temperate (Daggerfall) | 37.5 / 255 | 7 771 | `[24, 60, 49, 29]` | 1 123 |
| desert (Rihad) | 60.8 / 255 | 12 219 | `[41, 34, 12, 56]` | 1 154 |
| mountain (Eldenroot) | 41.6 / 255 | 13 651 | `[37, 22, 9, 32]` | 1 753 |

* The pictures differ (spread 23.2 of 255, colour counts 7.7 k → 13.7 k) — **and so does the
  country itself**: the three windows share *no* block, and place 1 123 / 1 154 / 1 753 flats.
  Climate is not a palette here, it selects terrain records. The wilderness bit of the
  complaint is therefore **not** reproduced as stated.
* What is still missing is exactly what the brief's own P4 lists, and none of it is claimed:
  no relief (heights, coasts, rivers, sea plane), no roads, no continuous Tamriel, no far
  ring/haze — the window is 128×128 cells around a cell and the horizon is the fog. The
  climate *art* (sky, palette) is there; the *landscape* is still Arena's flat wilderness.

## S-E — "persistent world features missing" — **the brief's premise is stale; a real gap remains**

Measured with the new instrument `__debug.navigations` (`src/main.js`, `sessionStorage`-based,
0 in the first document of a tab and +1 per page load after it):

* Standing in Daggerfall at 10:00, `teleportToDoor('equipment')` then a real `E`:
  **`navigations` stayed at 2 and a variable set on the street page (`window.__auditMark =
  4242`) was still readable inside `EQUIP2.MIF`.**
* So **a door is not a page load**, and neither are gates or stairs: `enterDoor`, `leaveDoor`,
  `crossGate` and `useStair` all go through `goInPlace` (`interior.js`), which re-boots the
  scene inside the same document. The brief's S35 — "a building is entered and left by a page
  load" — is **stale** and `docs/PROGRESS.md` §5 must be corrected in the same commit as the
  next change to that area (D-05a: a doc row that describes a mechanism the code no longer
  has).
* **What is genuinely missing is the world inside the document.** A hop is a full *scene
  re-boot* — decode, mesh, upload, populate — not a payload swap into a living world: the
  crowd, the clock, the kills and the loot in memory are rebuilt from the save file, never
  carried. That is D-A/P3, unchanged, and this probe is where its success is measured.

## The unexplained one (no defect filed — evidence only)

The first version of the probe watched the crowd on a *live* city page after
`__debug.setClock(1380)` and **hung for four minutes** with no answer and no error; the same
read on a page booted at 23:00 (`?clock=1380`) returns fine. The audit was changed to boot a
page rather than move the clock — a fresh page is the shape that cannot hang — and the
observation is recorded here rather than turned into a claim. If it repeats, it is a real
defect in the clock/sky recompose path; if it does not, it was the probe's own promise.

## The other suites, and two failures that were already there

A subset run of the suites that touch what this pass touched —
`node tests/run-browser.mjs testmap interior city townsfolk living doors world` — came to
**287 / 289 checks**. Both reds were in `townsfolk`, and both were **attributed, not assumed**:

* **"a walk cycle belongs to walking: no batch animates while standing still" — 46 of 48
  batches animate while the crowd is standing still.** Run at the baseline commit (`2986324`,
in a throwaway `git worktree` so nothing in the working tree moved) it fails *identically*, so
this pass did not introduce it: **D-08**, pre-existing, and the same family as the 23:00 freeze
(D-01a) — a crowd that is not walking, drawn with frames that are.
* **"T opens a conversation with the person in front of the player" — a stale assertion.** The
check demanded the heading match `/a person in /`, which is the *unnamed* form; since S30 closed
a heading reads `Elistan Mosswood — a man of the town`. The panel's prose, its `template.dat`
source and its topics all read correctly in the same run. The test was wrong, not `T`; repaired
to accept the two shapes the game can produce and logged as **D-10** (the five laws allow
changing a check only when it is *proved* wrong and written down). `townsfolk` alone is now
**56/57**, its one remaining red being D-08.

The remaining browser suites were **not run in this pass** — a green subset is not a full green,
and nothing here should be read as one.
